Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026 · This text applies to the Windows, Mac, Linux and Android (including Google Play) versions and to this website.

In short: The Tafsir & Hadith Library asks for no account; it does not collect your name, e-mail, contacts, location or device identifier. Your notes, highlights, memorization records and recordings stay on your device only. Apart from anonymous counts showing which parts of the program are used and for how long, nothing is sent, and these counts can be turned off with one tap in the settings.

1. Who is responsible?

The app is developed by Fehim Dadük and Kerem Uzuner and distributed free of charge. For any question about this policy: tefsirhadiskutuphanesi@gmail.com

2. Information not collected

  • Name, e-mail, phone number or any account information (the app has no accounts).
  • The texts you read, search or write; your notes, tags, highlights, bookmarks, memorization and khatm plans.
  • Location (GPS), contacts, photos, calendar, SMS or call information.
  • An advertising ID or any persistent identifier of the device. The app has no ads.

3. Anonymous usage statistics

To improve the program, only the following is sent, as counts that do not identify anyone:

InformationWhy
The part that is open (Mushaf, Tafsir, Hadith, Commentary, Dictionary, Search, Notes, Qur'an reading…)To see which parts are used
How long the program stays open (a “still open” count every few minutes while it is in front)To see the average time of use
Program version, operating system, interface language, screen sizeTo know which versions and devices to prioritize
Country (roughly derived from the connection address; the address itself is not stored)To see in which countries the program is used

These counts are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to the privacy-first, cookie-free Umami service (cloud.umami.is). Umami does not store IP addresses; the records cannot be linked to a person or device. A temporary key that links one session together is kept in memory only and erased when the program closes. This information is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone.

To turn them off: in the program, Display › Anonymous usage statistics › Off. While off, no counts are sent.

4. When the app connects to the internet

The program works offline. It connects only in these cases, and sends nothing about you in them:

  • Data packs: The tafsir, hadith and other data packs are downloaded from GitHub (github.com).
  • Version check: A small version file is read to find out whether there is a new version. The Google Play version gets its updates only through Google Play.
  • Recitation: The recitations you listen to or download are fetched from everyayah.com.

Like any web server, these services may briefly log the IP address a request comes from, under their own rules.

5. Permissions (Android)

PermissionWhat for
Internet, network stateDownloading data packs and recitations, sync between devices
Microphone (only when you ask)Recording your own recitation in the memorization section. The recording stays on your device and is sent nowhere.

When you share a verse card, the image is saved to your device's gallery (Pictures/Tefsir ve Hadis) and given only to the app you choose.

6. Sync between devices

When you sync your notes between two devices, nothing goes to the internet: they are transferred directly between two devices on the same Wi-Fi network, with a one-time code shown on screen.

7. Error and source reports

The “Report an error”, “Report a chain” and “Copyright notice” buttons only open your own e-mail app with a filled-in draft. Whether to send it is up to you; if you do, your e-mail is used only to answer the report.

8. This website

This website is published on GitHub Pages and uses the same cookie-free Umami service to count visits. The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts.

9. Children

The app is suitable for readers of every age, and since it collects personal information from no one, it collects none from children.

10. Storage and deletion of data

Your data is only on your own device: it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. As the anonymous counts cannot be linked to anyone, they cannot be separated and deleted per person; they remain in Umami as aggregate numbers.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new text is published on this page with a new effective date.
