Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026 · This text applies to the Windows, Mac, Linux, Android (including Google Play) and iOS/iPadOS (App Store) versions and to this website.

In short: The Tafsir & Hadith Library asks for no account; it does not collect your name, e-mail, contacts, location or the device's hardware identifier. Your notes, highlights, memorization records and recordings stay on your device only. Apart from anonymous counts showing which parts of the program are used, for how long and on how many separate devices, nothing is sent; these counts can be turned off in Settings.

1. Who is responsible?

The app is developed by Fehim Dadük and Kerem Uzuner and distributed free of charge. For any question about this policy: tefsirhadiskutuphanesi@gmail.com

2. Information not collected

  • Name, e-mail, phone number or any account information (the app has no accounts).
  • The texts you read, search or write; your notes, tags, highlights, bookmarks, memorization and khatm plans (only how many there are is counted, never their content: section 3).
  • Location (GPS), contacts, photos, calendar, SMS or call information.
  • An advertising ID or any identifier taken from the device (such as its serial number, hardware ID or phone number). The app has no ads.

These counts are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) only to the project's own counter running on Cloudflare (tefsir-sayac.tefsirkutuphanesi.workers.dev). The counter does not store IP addresses either; each start of the program carries a random mark kept in memory only. The anonymous device number is kept in the program's local settings folder on that device (not with your synced notes); if it is deleted, the program draws a new one. It cannot be matched with your name, your e-mail or your device's own identifiers. The website counts visits the same way, without cookies (the page opened, time on the page, clicks on the download buttons). Only the developers see the counts. This information is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone.

These counts contain no personal data and cannot be linked to anyone; they are used to see which parts of the program are useful and to improve it. If you do not want them, turn off the usage statistics option in Settings: while it is off the program sends no counts and makes no device number.

4. When the app connects to the internet

The program works offline. It connects only in these cases, and sends nothing about you in them:

  • Data packs: The tafsir, hadith and other data packs are downloaded from GitHub (github.com).
  • Version check: A small version file is read to find out whether there is a new version. The Google Play version gets its updates only through Google Play.
  • Recitation: The recitations you listen to or download are fetched from everyayah.com.

Like any web server, these services may briefly log the IP address a request comes from, under their own rules.

6. Sync between devices

With a backup file: you can save all your records in one backup file (.zip) and carry it to another device yourself. The file is saved only where you choose; it is not sent to us.

With Google Drive (optional): if you choose «Connect with Google», your notes, highlights, bookmarks, hadith and fiqh notes, memorisation, recitation-tracking and khatm records are kept as one file in your own Google Drive, in the hidden «application data» folder Google sets aside for apps, and synced across the devices you connect with the same account.

  • The app asks only for the drive.appdata permission: it cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.
  • Your account's e-mail address is kept on your device only to show which account is connected.
  • The connection is directly between your device and Google; your data never goes to a server of ours and we cannot see it.
  • The access token Google issues is stored only on your device. «Disconnect» deletes it and revokes the permission; you can also remove it under Security › Third-party access in your Google account.
  • To delete the sync file from Drive, use Settings › Manage apps › Tafsir & Hadith Library › Delete hidden app data in Google Drive.

Tafsir & Hadith Library's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This information is used only for syncing; it is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with anyone and not used to train AI models.

7. Error and source reports

The “Report an error”, “Report a chain” and “Copyright notice” buttons only open your own e-mail app with a filled-in draft. Whether to send it is up to you; if you do, your e-mail is used only to answer the report.

8. This website

This website is published on GitHub Pages and counts visits with the same counter, without cookies. The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts.

9. Children

The app is suitable for readers of every age, and since it collects personal information from no one, it collects none from children.

10. Storage and deletion of data

Your data is only on your own device: it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. As the anonymous counts cannot be linked to anyone, they cannot be separated and deleted per person; they remain in the counter as aggregate numbers.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new text is published on this page with a new effective date.
